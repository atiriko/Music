Open Music Maker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Your music is created on your device. You do not need an account, and we do not receive your songs or imported instruments.

About this policy

This policy covers Open Music Maker on iOS, iPadOS, Android, and the website at music.loomworkz.com. Open Music Maker is developed by Atahan Sahlan. In this policy, “we” means Atahan Sahlan.

Music and files stay on your device

Composition, playback, and MIDI and WAV rendering happen locally. The app processes your music settings, song arrangements, project names, and files you choose to import, including song projects and SoundFont (.sf2) instrument libraries. These are used to provide the features you request, not uploaded to our servers or sent to an external AI service.

The installed mobile app includes its engine and instrument assets. The web version downloads these assets from the website to run in your browser; those requests do not upload your compositions.

Local storage and backups

The app saves your current draft, saved projects, preferences, and imported instrument libraries on your device so they can be restored when you return. The website uses browser storage, including local storage and IndexedDB. Mobile versions use app preferences and local app storage, including IndexedDB for imported libraries.

We do not operate a cloud account or sync service. Your operating system or device backup provider may include app data in backups according to your settings. Those backups are controlled by you and the provider.

No advertising or tracking

The app does not include advertising networks, analytics SDKs, tracking cookies, or an account registration system. We do not sell your personal information, use your music for advertising, or collect your music for training AI models.

The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access. Importing a file gives the app access to the file you select; it does not upload the file to us.

Website requests and technical logs

When you visit the website or this policy page, the web server and hosting or network providers process connection information needed to deliver the page. Server access and error logs can include your IP address, request time, requested URL, response status, referring page, and browser or device information sent by your browser.

This information is used to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the website. It does not contain the locally stored contents of your songs or instrument libraries. Log retention depends on the hosting service’s log rotation and security settings; it is separate from your on-device music storage. Contact us if you need details or wish to request deletion of information associated with a website visit.

Exporting and sharing

Exports are created on your device. The web version downloads the chosen file through your browser. Mobile versions write export files to the app’s cache and open the operating system’s share sheet. Files are shared only with the destination you choose.

If you choose a cloud drive, email service, or another app, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. App stores and device providers may also process installation, purchase, backup, or diagnostic information under their own policies and your settings. The app does not send your compositions to those providers automatically.

Retention and deletion

  • Saved projects: delete individual saved songs in Projects. Deleting a saved copy does not necessarily clear the current working draft.
  • Imported instruments: remove imported libraries using the app’s library controls.
  • All local website data: clear this website’s site data in your browser settings to remove drafts, projects, preferences, and imported libraries.
  • All mobile app data: use Android’s Clear storage option, or delete the app on iOS or iPadOS. Offloading an iOS app preserves its data. Reinstalling or restoring a backup may restore previously saved data.
  • Exports and backups: delete downloaded files, shared copies, and device or cloud backups separately using the relevant app or provider.

Local data remains until you remove it or your device or browser clears it. Export caches may remain until cleared by the operating system or by removing app data. We cannot access or remotely delete your on-device files, and there is no account to delete.

Security and your choices

The public website uses HTTPS. Local data is protected by your browser’s or operating system’s storage controls. Keep your device secure and choose sharing destinations carefully; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Where applicable, you can ask about personal information we hold, request access, correction or deletion, or object to its use. This concerns information we actually receive, such as website logs or correspondence, rather than music that stays on your device. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. It has no account or social features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact Atahan Sahlan using the developer contact shown on the Open Music Maker App Store or Google Play listing. If you contact us, we receive the contact details and message you provide and use them to respond. Correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet any applicable legal obligations.

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
